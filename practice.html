Practice Sheet

The practice sheet is the official training material for AUBM's ICPC candidates during the 2026 practice period. It introduces the C++ language and the fundamentals of competitive programming one day at a time, in preparation for the selection contest.

About the program

This program assumes no prior programming experience. The language used throughout the practice material is C++ only. Using C++ is a significant advantage in the contest, so it is strongly encouraged: at the last ICPC, 98.5% of submissions were written in C++.

Each day introduces a small number of concepts. Every concept is explained step by step with worked examples, and each day contains practice problems and homework problems on the AUBM ICPC group on Codeforces.

How to use this material

Practice problems

Read the problem and try to come up with a solution in your head first. Write the code if you can, then go through the walk-through for that problem on this website. Practice problems are meant for learning, so you may use AI to have concepts or code explained to you.

Homework problems

Solutions to homework problems are not posted here. Do your best to solve them on your own. If you are new to C++, use AI only for help with syntax, not to solve the problem for you. If you cannot solve a homework problem, it is better to skip it and come back to it later than to let AI give you a big hint or solve it for you.

Keep in mind that AI will not be allowed during the actual contest.
