What is the ICPC?

The International Collegiate Programming Contest is the oldest and largest programming competition for university students. Teams of three share one computer and have five hours to solve around a dozen algorithmic problems.

Each problem describes a task your program has to solve. You submit your code and it's tested automatically on hidden inputs, so it has to be both correct and fast. The team that solves the most problems wins, with total time as the tiebreaker. Teams move up through four stages.

  1. ICPC Cyprus

    National

    Each university's best team qualifies for SEERC.

    24 October, Paphos

  2. SEERC

    Southeastern Europe regional

    Universities from 13 countries. The top teams advance.

  3. European Championship

    Europe

    The best teams from Europe's regionals compete for World Finals places.

  4. World Finals

    Global

    The top university teams from around the world.
